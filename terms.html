What this covers

shortbot is a personal program run by one individual on their own hardware to operate a single YouTube channel that they own. It is not offered to anyone else. There is no service to subscribe to, no account to create, and no customer relationship for these terms to govern.

These terms exist because Google requires a published terms link for an OAuth application, and stating the situation plainly is more honest than reproducing boilerplate written for a product that has users.

Who may use it

Its operator, on their own account. Access is granted by that operator's own OAuth consent and can be revoked by them at any time from Google account permissions.

What it does

  • Generates short videos from licensed stock footage and synthetic narration.
  • Uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel, marked as containing synthetic media.
  • Is being extended to read that channel's own analytics, and its own videos' comments, in order to adjust how it makes subsequent videos. Those parts are not finished and request no permissions yet.

How it is separated

It runs as two programs with separate credentials: one writes videos and cannot publish, one publishes and contains no language model. This is the design's main safety property and it is described in the privacy policy.

What it will not do

  • Claim to be a human being, or deny being software when asked.
  • Use footage or music it is not licensed to use.
  • Present invented facts as true. Factual claims are checked before a video is made, and anything that cannot be supported is removed.
  • Publish a title that misrepresents what the video contains.
  • Remove or hide comments for being critical, or for pointing out that the channel is automated. Moderation is limited to spam and harassment.

No warranty

The program is provided as-is by its operator to themselves. There is no warranty, and no liability is accepted to anyone, because there is nobody else party to it.
