shortbot

A personal automation tool that runs one YouTube Shorts channel.

What it is

shortbot is a single-user program. It generates short videos from licensed stock footage and synthetic narration and uploads them to one YouTube channel owned by its operator. It is being built to read that channel's own analytics and adjust how it makes the next video; that part is not finished.

It runs as two separate programs holding separate credentials. The one that writes videos cannot publish them, and the one that publishes has no language model in it. See the privacy policy for why.

It is not a product, not a service, and has no users other than its operator. There is no sign-up, because there is nothing to sign up to.

It is openly machine-run

The channel it operates does not pretend to be a person. Every video it uploads is marked as containing synthetic media using YouTube's own disclosure field, the channel description states that it is software-run, and if a viewer asks whether it is a bot, it says yes.

What it accesses

It authenticates as its operator, to that operator's own YouTube channel, and nothing else. It does not read, collect, or process data belonging to anyone other than its operator. The full list of permissions it requests, and what each is for, is in the privacy policy.
